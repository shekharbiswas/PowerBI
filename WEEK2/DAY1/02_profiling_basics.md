# 📊 Data Profiling Basics & Spotting Bad Data

Now that you understand the ETL pipeline and staging area, it’s time to explore a crucial step before analysis: **profiling your data**.

Data profiling helps you **catch errors before they become embarrassing mistakes** in reports. You'll spot duplicates, missing values, outliers, and inconsistencies — all using built-in Power BI tools.

<br>

## 🎯 Goal for This Session

Learn how to assess data quality using **Power Query’s data profiling features**, and get hands-on practice spotting and fixing issues.

<br>

## 🧠 Why This Matters

Even if your data looks good on the surface, hidden issues like blank fields or bad types can cause:
- 🚫 Broken charts  
- ❌ Incorrect results  
- 🤯 Confusion for stakeholders

> A professional analyst always checks data before building visuals. **Profiling is your first line of defense.**
