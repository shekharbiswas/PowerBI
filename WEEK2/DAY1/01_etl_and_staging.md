# 🔄 Day 1A: Understanding ETL & the Power BI Staging Area

Welcome to Week 2! You're now moving beyond just connecting and transforming data — it’s time to think like a data engineer.

Today, we focus on the **Load** step of the ETL process and introduce the concept of a **staging area** — where data is held before it’s fully modeled and visualized.

<br>

## 🎯 Goal for This Session

Understand the full **ETL pipeline**, how Power BI handles data behind the scenes, and what the **staging area** means in real-world data projects.

<br>

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- What “ETL” really means in Power BI  
- How the staging area supports modular and reusable workflows  
- Why separating loading from transforming makes your reports cleaner and faster

> Think of staging like a **waiting room for data** — it’s cleaned up before being sent to the final model.
