# 🧩 Managing Files, Global Options & Fine-Tuning Queries

In this session, you’ll learn how to **fine-tune your queries** for better control, especially when working with **multiple files, folders, or teams**.

These options may seem small, but they make a **big difference** when scaling your Power BI reports.

<br>

## 🎯 Goal for this session

- Learn how to manage Power BI file-level settings  
- Understand **global options** for data loading and privacy  
- Practice final tweaks that improve reliability and performance

<br>

## 🧠 Why This Matters

As your reports grow:
- You’ll import from **multiple folders**  
- You’ll work with **external collaborators**  
- You’ll want to **avoid manual cleanup every single time**

Power BI offers global and query-level controls to help you **build smarter and safer workflows**.

> Great analysts aren’t just builders — they’re maintainers. These tweaks help you avoid hidden errors later.
