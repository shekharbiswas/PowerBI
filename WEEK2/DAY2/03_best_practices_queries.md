# ⚙️ Power Query best practices & performance tips

Welcome to Day 2! Today is all about **working smarter with Power Query**. You’ll explore best practices that help you write cleaner queries, troubleshoot issues, and make your reports run faster — especially as data grows.

<br>

## 🎯 Goal for This Session

Learn **best practices** when working with Power Query, including structure, reusability, and performance optimization.

<br>

## 🧠 Why This Matters

Early in your data career, it's tempting to "just get things to work." But as datasets grow and teams rely on your reports, performance starts to matter — **a lot**.

> Slow dashboards, complex queries, and messy logic are the top reasons projects fail in production.

## 💡 Real-Life Advice

> 🚀 Reports that run in 3 seconds get used. Reports that take 30 seconds? Usually ignored.

Following best practices early saves time later — and helps others understand and reuse your work.

<br>

## ✅ Quick Checklist for Better Power Query

- Break big queries into smaller steps ✅  
- Use **reference queries** to avoid duplication ✅  
- Keep column types consistent ✅  
- Rename steps for clarity ✅  
- Use fewer transformations inside visuals — clean data first ✅
