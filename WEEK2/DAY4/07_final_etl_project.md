## 💡 Final Project Tip

> 🧠 Focus on clarity, not complexity.  
A clean, simple transformation pipeline is often better than a clever one. Keep your steps logical, modular, and well-named.

Use everything you’ve learned:
- Profiling before transforming  
- Avoiding duplication with reference queries  
- Choosing the right data types  
- Building reusable steps

<br>
