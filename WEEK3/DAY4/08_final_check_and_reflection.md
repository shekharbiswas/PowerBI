## 🛠️ Final mini-project: Clean, shareable report

### Goal:
Polish your interactive sales report and create a professional dashboard.

### Steps:
1. Open your optimized report (`SalesReport_Optimized.pbix`)  
2. Review for layout consistency and theme alignment  
3. Ensure all slicers, tooltips, and interactivity work smoothly  
4. Pin final visuals to a dashboard and test responsiveness  
5. Export a PDF or share a link with a peer or mentor  
6. Save your final files with clear names like `Final_SalesReport.pbix` and `Sales_Dashboard.pdf`
