# 📤 Sharing and publishing reports (Module 3 – Part 2)

In this session, you’ll learn how to **publish your reports** to the Power BI Service, **export them** for sharing, and manage how stakeholders access your work.

Publishing and sharing are where your reports come to life. You'll also explore **pagination**, **URLs**, and **analyze in Excel** for deeper reporting extensions.

## 🧠 What you’ll learn

- How to publish Power BI reports to the web or workspace

- How to share live dashboards with stakeholders

<br>

## 🖼️ Key concepts

| Concept                | Description                                                             |
|------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Publishing reports** | Upload PBIX files to Power BI Service for cloud access and sharing      |
| **Exporting**          | Save reports as PDF, PowerPoint, or embed in other tools                |
| **Analyze in Excel**   | Explore published datasets from Excel using a live connection           |
| **URLs and links**     | Use hyperlinks or buttons to connect users to deeper report pages       |
| **Permissions**        | Control who can see, edit, or share your published content              |

## 🛠️ Practice task: Publish and share your report

### Goal:
Make your interactive sales report available to others.

### Steps:
1. Revisit your `SalesReport_Interactive.pbix`  
2. Publish it to Power BI Service (if not done already)  
3. Export one page as PDF  
4. Try the "Analyze in Excel" option and connect to your dataset  
5. Create a **report sharing link** or embed URL  
6. Save and organize your files in workspace folders
