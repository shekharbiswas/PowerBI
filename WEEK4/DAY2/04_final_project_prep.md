## 🛠️ Practice task: Finalize executive summary

### Goal:
Turn the draft into a polished, shareable summary of Capital Bikeshare's performance.

### Checklist:
- [ ] Report has a clear title and layout
- [ ] 3–5 visuals max (including AI-driven)
- [ ] At least 1 KPI card or trend line
- [ ] Explanatory callouts or labels for each visual
- [ ] The forecast shown is the revisited version, and its callout explains why in one plain sentence
- [ ] Insights are business-focused, not overly technical
- [ ] Consistent theme and colors used throughout
- [ ] Exported as `Final_BikeshareExecutiveSummary.pdf` or `.pbix`
