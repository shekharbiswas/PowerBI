## 📋 Case summary: Adventure Works

📌 You are tasked with creating a concise, **data-driven summary** of Adventure Works' business performance.

### Executive needs:
- High-level visuals (KPIs, decomposition tree, key influencers)  
- Forecasts and explanations of changes  
- Storytelling layout: **What happened? Why? What now?**

<br>

## 🛠️ Practice task: Initial case exploration

### Goal:
Use AI visuals to identify key drivers and performance trends.

### Steps:
1. Load the **Adventure Works dataset** (provided in Coursera resources or your repo)  
2. Create 3 core visuals:  
   - **Key Influencers** for identifying top sales drivers  
   - **Decomposition Tree** for root cause analysis  
   - **Forecast** for a time-based metric  
3. Add supporting visuals (card, bar chart, Q&A visual)  
4. Take notes for what should go into your **executive summary**  
5. Save as `AdventureWorks_AI_Exploration.pbix`
