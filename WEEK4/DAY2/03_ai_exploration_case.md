## 📋 Case summary: Capital Bikeshare

📌 We are tasked with creating a concise, **data-driven summary** of Capital Bikeshare's ridership performance across the full available history.

### Executive needs:
- High-level visuals (KPIs, decomposition tree, key influencers)
- A forecast, and a plain explanation of why it looks the way it does
- Storytelling layout: **What happened? Why? What now?**

> A note on scope: yesterday we found a stretch of the history that didn't behave like the rest, and we rebuilt the forecast to account for it. An executive reading a three-minute summary will not see the raw data the way we did — if that stretch matters to the story, we decide how much of it belongs on the page, and how it's explained.
