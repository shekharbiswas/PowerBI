# 📊 Overview: Analysis, AI visuals & final project

Welcome to **Week 4** — your final week in the Power BI learning journey with **Dataskools**.  
This week, you’ll learn how to go beyond visuals and start **analyzing data patterns**, building forecasts, and using **AI-powered visuals** in Power BI.

You’ll also complete a final capstone-style project and reflect on how far you've come.

## 🎯 Learning objectives

By the end of this week, you will be able to:

- Perform statistical and trend analysis using Power BI  
- Apply advanced visuals including forecasting and key influencers  
- Use AI-powered visuals to support root cause analysis  
- Complete a final business report and executive summary  
- Reflect on your journey and plan your next steps as a Power BI user

## 📈 Difficulty snapshot – Week 4

| 🧠 Topic Area             | ⚙️ Difficulty | 💬 What to Expect                                                    |
|---------------------------|---------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 📊 Clustering & binning    | 🟡 Medium      | Involves understanding group logic and category breakdowns           |
| 📈 Forecasting             | 🔴 Hard        | Requires time-based data and interpretation of prediction ranges     |
| 🤖 AI visuals              | 🟡 Medium      | User-friendly but conceptually deep in how Power BI interprets data |
| 📋 Executive summary       | 🟢 Easy        | Focused on storytelling, layout, and clarity                         |
| 🧠 Reflection & planning   | 🟢 Easy        | Thoughtful review, no technical difficulty                           |
