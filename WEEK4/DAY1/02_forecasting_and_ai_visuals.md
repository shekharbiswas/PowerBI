## 🖼️ Key concepts

| Tool/Visual              | Purpose                                                                     |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Forecasting**          | Predict future values using trends in time series data                      |
| **Reference lines**      | Add markers for goals, targets, or statistical thresholds                   |
| **Error bars**           | Show uncertainty or confidence intervals in visuals                         |
| **Key Influencers**      | Discover which fields impact a selected value the most                     |
| **Decomposition Tree**   | Drill down into metrics to understand **why** a change occurred             |
| **Q&A Visual**           | Enter natural language questions to generate charts automatically           |

## 🛠️ Practice task: Explain a trend with AI tools

### Goal:
Use forecasting and AI visuals to tell the story behind your data.

### Steps:
1. Reopen your grouped or clustered report from earlier (`PatternAnalysis_Grouping.pbix`)  
2. Create a **forecasting line** for a time-based metric (e.g., revenue or units sold)  
3. Add a **reference line** for your sales goal  
4. Apply **error bars** to visualize variability  
5. Insert a **Key Influencers** visual to analyze drivers of change  
6. Use a **Decomposition Tree** to break down sales by region, product, or segment  
7. Try a **Q&A visual** to ask "What drives high revenue?"  
8. Save as `PatternAnalysis_AI.pbix`
