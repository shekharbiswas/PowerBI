## Block 2 — The Exam Blueprint, In Detail (1 Hour)

### Step 1 — The Four Domains and Their Weights (20 mins)

The exam splits into four weighted areas:

| Exam Topic | Weight |
|---|---|
| Prepare data | 15–20% |
| Model data | 30–35% |
| Visualize and analyze data | 25–30% |
| Deploy and maintain solutions | 20–25% |

Notice that modelling — relationships, DAX, filter context — carries the heaviest weight of any domain. Write in our notes: does our Block 1 self-assessment line up with where the exam actually puts its weight? If modelling was our weakest area, that mismatch matters more than a weak spot in a lightly-weighted domain.

---

### Step 2 — What Each Domain Actually Involves (25 mins)

**Prepare data:** connecting to Excel, CSV, databases, or web data; cleaning in Power Query; removing errors, filtering rows, fixing types; merging and appending; profiling for missing values or duplicates.

**Model data:** building relationships (one-to-many, many-to-many); understanding filter direction and cardinality; writing DAX measures with `SUM`, `CALCULATE`, `FILTER`, and time functions; calculated columns, hierarchies, table context; filter context versus row context.

**Visualize and analyze data:** bar charts, line charts, maps, slicers, KPI cards; bookmarks, drillthroughs, filters; AI visuals — Key Influencers, Decomposition Trees, Q&A; conditional formatting and themes; basic statistical analysis and forecasting.

**Deploy and maintain:** publishing to Power BI Service; managing datasets and workspace permissions; row-level security; scheduled refresh and performance monitoring; usage metrics and versioning.

For each domain, write one real example from our own capstone work that demonstrates it — for instance, the star schema built in Week 4 Day 3 for modelling, or the Key Influencers work from Week 4 Day 2 for visualize and analyze. If a domain has no real example from our own work, that's a gap worth naming plainly.

---

### Step 3 — Question Format (15 mins)

Note in our notes what to expect: multiple-choice, scenario-based questions, and interactive formats like drag-and-drop or choosing the right visual type for a scenario. This isn't pure recall — it's applied judgement, closer to the capstone brief than a vocabulary quiz.

## Block 3 — Build a Personal Study Plan (1.5 Hours)

### Step 1 — Choose a Pace (15 mins)

Two general paths:

| Prep Style | Time Needed | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Focused, ~3 hrs/day | 8 weeks | Solid Power BI foundation, wants a steady, thorough pace |
| Fast track | 4 weeks | Already confident across all four domains, wants to move quickly |

Pick one based on the honest self-assessment from Block 1 — not the pace that sounds most impressive. Write the choice and the reason for it in our notes.

---

### Step 2 — Weight the Plan Toward Our Actual Gaps (30 mins)

Using the domain weights from Block 2 and our own self-assessment, allocate study time so the heaviest, weakest areas get the most hours — not equal time across all four domains regardless of need.

If data modelling and DAX are a weak spot, plan for extra time here specifically — this is consistently the domain that takes learners longest, even after a programme like this one.

Write a rough weekly breakdown: which domain gets focus each week, and roughly how many hours.
