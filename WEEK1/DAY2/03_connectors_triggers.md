# 🔌 Connectors, Triggers & Automation

Now that you know how to load flat files and choose storage modes, it’s time to explore how Power BI **connects to hundreds of data services** and how you can **automate actions** with triggers.

<br>

## 🎯 Goal for today

Understand how Power BI interacts with other tools, and how to trigger updates and automate workflows using actions.

<br>

## 🧠 Why this matters

Let’s say your marketing team updates a Google Sheet every morning with campaign spend. Do you really want to reload the report manually every day?

Here’s where **connectors** and **triggers** shine. You can:
- **Pull in data** from SharePoint, SQL, Salesforce, even Twitter
- **Automate actions** like refreshing a dashboard when data changes
- **Create workflows** that save you time and reduce human error

> 🛠️ The more you automate, the more time you’ll have to focus on analysis, not manual work.
