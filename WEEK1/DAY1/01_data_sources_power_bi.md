## 🧠 Why This Matters

Every dashboard starts with **understanding your data source**. If you can't access or trust your data, your insights will be weak. That's why Power BI makes it easy to pull from many types of data — flat files, databases, cloud sources — and gives you control over how that data is stored and refreshed.
